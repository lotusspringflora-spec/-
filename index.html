名詞片語

Noun Phrases ／ 不定量 · 容器量詞 · 片語組成

名詞片語 1/2

不定量片語：可數／不可數是關鍵

片語意思例句
a lot of / plenty of許多（可數＋不可數）We have plenty of time.
a number of許多（＋可數複數）A number of students are absent.
a great deal of大量（＋不可數）He spent a great deal of money.
the rest of其餘的The rest of the food is for you.
one of the + 最高級最…之一one of the best movies
none of沒有一個None of the answers is right.
a few / few（可數）
  • a few = 一些（肯定）
  • few = 幾乎沒有（否定）
  • I have a few questions.
VS
a little / little（不可數）
  • a little = 一些（肯定）
  • little = 幾乎沒有（否定）
  • There is a little milk.
名詞片語 2/2

✏️ 重點：不可數名詞要表達數量，一定要加量詞。 water → a bottle of water；advice → a piece of advice
片語組成：限定詞 ＋（形容詞）＋ 名詞
my big blue box｜the young man｜a used car
the + 形容詞 = ～的人（當複數）：the rich、the poor、the blind

Phrasal Verbs ／ look · take · get · give · put · turn…

動詞片語 1/4

look 系列 vs take 系列

look 系列意思take 系列意思
look for尋找take care of照顧
look after照顧take part in參加
look up查（字典）take off起飛／脫掉
look forward to期待take place發生（不用被動）
look out小心take pride in以…為榮
I'm looking for my keys.　我在找我的鑰匙。
Look up the word in the dictionary.　查字典裡這個字。
The plane will take off at 3:00.　飛機三點起飛。
The story took place in Japan.　故事發生在日本。
🎯 考點：look forward to 的 to 是介係詞，後面要接 V-ing：look forward to seeing you

動詞片語 3/4

come · break · run 與 be made 系列

片語意思例句
come up with想出We came up with a good plan.
come true實現Your dream will come true.
break down故障／崩潰My bike broke down on the way.
run out of用完We ran out of paper.
hand in交出（作業）Hand in your homework by Friday.
find out / work out查明／解出I need to find out the truth.
be made 的四種介係詞
  • made of — 看得出原料（桌子是木頭）
  • made from — 看不出原料（紙來自樹）
  • made in — 產於（made in Japan）
  • made into — 被製成（木頭做成紙）
🔑
速記口訣
  • 看得到原料 → of
  • 看不到原料 → from
  • 產地 → in
  • 原材料變成品 → into
動詞片語 4/4

動詞 ＋ 受詞 ＋ 補語：最容易錯的一頁

錯誤

❌ 常見錯誤寫法

ask me wait outside.
make him to stand up.
want she go home.
I'd like buy a ticket.
used to getting up early.
be used to get up early.
正確

✅ 正確用法

ask me to wait outside.　請某人做
make him stand up.　使某人做（原形）
want her to go home.　想要某人做
I'd like to buy a ticket.　想要做
used to get up early.　過去常常
be used to getting up early.　習慣於
← 拖曳滑桿，掃描線由上而下，「正確用法」逐行覆蓋「錯誤寫法」 →
✏️ 口訣：ask / want / tell / teach / allow → to V；make / let / have → 原形 V （被動要還原 to：be made to clean）；help → (to) V

介係詞片語

Prepositional Phrases ／ 時間 · 地點 · 交通 · 常用片語

介係詞片語 1/2

時間 vs 地點介係詞：用法一次分清

時間

⏰ 時間：由小到大

at + 點鐘　　at 6:00 / at night
on + 星期、日期　on Monday / on May 1st
in + 年、月、季、上下午　in 2026 / in May
by + 時點　到…之前（完成）
for + 期間　for three years（持續）
地點

📍 地點：由大到小

in + 大地方　in Taipei / in the room
at + 小地點　at the station / at school
on + 平面、樓層　on the wall / on the 3F
between A and B　兩者之間
in front of / behind　前／後（外部）
← 拖曳滑桿，掃描線由上而下，「地點介係詞」逐行覆蓋「時間介係詞」 →
My birthday is on May 5th.｜He was born in 2010.｜The meeting starts at 9:00.
At the age of ten, he could play the piano.
介係詞片語 2/2

交通說法 ＋ 常考介係詞片語

🚗 交通
  • by bus / car / train / plane（不加冠詞）
  • on a bus / a train（強調某一台）
  • in a car / a taxi（在車內）
  • on foot 步行
  • go to school by bike ≠ ride a bike
🚌
🧩 常考片語
  • by the way 順帶一提
  • in fact 事實上
  • at least 至少／at last 最後
  • on time 準時／in time 及時
  • in danger 危險中／in trouble 遇麻煩
🗣️
in + 語文

🛠️
by + V-ing

❗
because / because of

形容詞片語 ＋ 副詞片語

形容詞片語（be + adj + 介係詞）意思副詞／連接副詞意思
be good at擅長first of all首先
be famous for以…聞名at least / at once至少／立刻
be interested in對…有興趣however然而
be different from與…不同therefore / as a result因此
be proud of / strict with以…為榮／對…嚴格instead / for example反而／例如
be popular with受…歡迎in addition此外
✏️ 易錯：however = but，但 however 可放句首（後加逗號），but 不行，兩者不可同時使用。
Taiwan is famous for its night markets.　台灣以夜市聞名。
It rained. However, we still went out.　下雨了，然而我們還是出去了。

會考必背句型

⏱️

⚖️
比較句型
too adj to V 太…而不能

the more…, the more…
🔗
對等連接詞
both A and B（複數）
either A or B（就近）
neither A nor B（就近）
not only A but also B
It took me two hours to finish the work.　我花兩小時完成工作。
The box is too heavy to carry.　這箱子太重搬不動。
Either you or I am wrong.　要嘛你、要嘛我錯了。（動詞與最近的主詞一致）
How about going hiking? / Why not try again?
互動環節

🔥 快問快答：點一下看答案

1. I can't find my keys. I'm still ________ them everywhere.
(A) looking after (B) looking up (C) looking for (D) looking forward to
答案 (C) looking for 尋找
2. The plane will ________ at 3:00 this afternoon.
(A) take place (B) take off (C) take part in (D) take care of
答案 (B) take off 起飛
3. The meeting was ________ till next Monday.
(A) put on (B) put up (C) put away (D) put off
答案 (D) put off 延後
4. She is good ________ mathematics.
(A) in (B) at (C) on (D) of
答案 (B) be good at 擅長
5. My mother often makes me ________ my room every day.
(A) to clean (B) clean (C) cleaning (D) cleans
答案 (B) make sb + 原形 V
6. I'm not used to ________ up so early in the morning.
(A) get (B) to get (C) getting (D) got
答案 (C) be used to + V-ing 習慣於
